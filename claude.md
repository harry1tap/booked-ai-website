- React (JSX)
- Tailwind CSS for styling

## Website Structure

The website is a single page with five sections in this order:

### 1. Hero (NSVP)
- Headline communicates the NSVP: "We give local service businesses the freedom to focus on their craft and their family — by building them a custom AI growth system that generates leads, books appointments, and makes them the go-to business in their area."
- Clear, punchy subtext
- Primary CTA button (book a call)
- This section grabs attention and clearly communicates what sets us apart

### 2. Problem
- Speak directly to the pain points of local service business owners
- Key pain points to address:
  - They don't know where to start with marketing, or what they've tried hasn't worked
  - They've stayed up until midnight watching YouTube tutorials and still can't get it right
  - They can't answer the phone — they're up a ladder, on a job, having dinner with their family
  - By the time they call back, the lead has gone with someone else
  - Monday mornings are spent chasing cold leads and calling people who've already gone elsewhere
  - Worrying about paying staff, making ends meet, where the next job is coming from
- Tone: empathetic but direct. We understand because we've been on the other side of the sales process

### 3. Solution / Process (3-Step Process)
- Simple, clear 3-step breakdown of how it works:
  - Step 1: We run targeted Meta ads to generate a consistent flow of enquiries from people who actually need the service
  - Step 2: Our custom-built AI qualifies every lead against the client's specific criteria, follows up instantly, pre-sells the appointment, and books it straight into the diary
  - Step 3: The client turns up to quote jobs with high-intent, pre-qualified prospects who are ready to move forward
- Keep it simple — no jargon, no waffle

### 4. What's Included / Dream Outcome
- Show what they get (the full service):
  - Targeted Meta ad campaigns
  - Custom-built AI booking agent (not white-label)
  - Website revamp
  - AI-powered missed call text back (WhatsApp + SMS)
  - Automated Google review collection
  - Weekly reports
  - Monthly strategy calls
  - Direct WhatsApp access
- Then paint the dream outcome:
  - Pipeline filling up automatically
  - Appointments booked in without lifting a finger
  - No more missed leads or cold follow-ups
  - Freedom to focus on doing the actual work and spending time with family
  - Becoming the highest-rated, most visible business in their area
  - Going from stressed tradesperson to market leader running an AI-first business

### 5. CTA (Final)
- Strong closing call-to-action
- Book a call / get started
- Reinforce: no false promises, no lock-ins, no BS

## Brand Guidelines

### Colour Palette — IGNITE
- Primary / Background: Black `#0D0D0D`
- Accent: Blaze Orange `#E07A2F`
- Contrast / Breathing room: White `#F5F5F5`
- **NEVER use blue** — deliberate brand decision to stand apart from every other AI/agency brand
- No gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects, no textures
- Flat design only
- Black backgrounds with orange elements as default
- Orange should pop against black — this is core to the visual identity

### Typography
- Primary font: **Outfit ExtraBold** (Google Fonts) for headings
- Body font: **Outfit Regular/Medium** for body text
- Fallback: Satoshi
- **NEVER use**: Montserrat, Poppins, Inter, Roboto, Arial, system fonts — overused and generic
- Import from Google Fonts

### Visual Style
- Simplicity with edge
- Professional without being corporate
- Bold without being gimmicky
- Minimal and clean
- If there are 100 white sheep, we're the black one
- No stock photography of people in suits or generic office settings
- Prefer icons, illustrations, or abstract elements if visuals are needed
- No decorative clutter — every element should serve a purpose

### Logo
- Wordmark: "Booked AI" — capital B, lowercase "ooked", space, capital "AI"
- Logo is being professionally designed separately — use text placeholder for now
- Use Outfit ExtraBold for the text placeholder

## Brand Voice Rules

These rules apply to ALL copy on the website:

- Direct, straight-talking, anti-agency
- Short sentences, punchy, no waffle
- Simple language — no jargon, no buzzwords, no corporate speak
- Confident but warm, not corporate
- **NEVER use em dashes (—) or en dashes (–) in website copy** — use commas, full stops, or restructure the sentence instead
- Never sound: salesy, desperate, generic, over-promising, corporate, or distant
- If it could come from any agency, it doesn't come from us
- If it makes the client feel like a number, we don't say it
- Speak like a real person talking to a real business owner
- Write like you're explaining it to a mate, not presenting to a boardroom

## Performance and Best Practices

- **Mobile-first responsive design** — must look and work perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Optimise for speed** — no large uncompressed images, lazy load where appropriate
- **Lightweight** — minimal dependencies, no bloated libraries
- **Accessible** — proper semantic HTML, alt text, contrast ratios, keyboard navigation
- **SEO basics** — proper meta tags, page title, description, Open Graph tags, clean heading hierarchy
- **No black hat SEO** — no keyword stuffing, no hidden text
- **Test with Google PageSpeed Insights** — aim for 90+ on both mobile and desktop
- **Smooth scroll** between sections
- **Clean, readable code** — well-structured components, consistent naming

## What NOT to Do

- No generic AI aesthetics (purple gradients, floating particles, generic tech vibes)
- No stock photos
- No blue anywhere
- No em dashes or en dashes in copy
- No corporate language
- No walls of text — keep sections scannable
- No fake testimonials or social proof — we have none yet and we don't lie
- No "guaranteed results" language on the public site
- No complex animations that slow the page down
- No cookie-cutter agency template feel
