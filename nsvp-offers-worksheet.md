## Defining Your Strengths

In this section, we'll explore the unique strengths that you contribute to your agency, outlining the skills, knowledge, and benefits that set it apart in a competitive landscape.

---

**What do you believe sets your agency apart from the competition?**

Three things. First, I've actually been on the other side of the sales process. I spent two years in inbound telesales selling private health insurance, calling leads, qualifying them, closing over the phone.

Before that, I was an estate agent doing face-to-face sales. I know what a good lead looks like because I've had to work them. I know what a bad one looks like because I've wasted hours on them. Most agency owners have never had to pick up the phone and sell — they're marketers, not salespeople. That means I build systems that deliver leads the way a salesperson would actually want to receive them: pre-qualified, informed, and ready to have a conversation.

Second, the AI side is entirely self-taught. I didn't buy a course on white-labelling some platform — I taught myself Python because I could see where things were heading and I was genuinely fascinated by it. That means our AI appointment agents are custom-built from the ground up, not some off-the-shelf tool with a logo on it. If a client needs something specific, I can build it. Most agencies can't say that.

Third, I'm direct and accessible. Business owners don't want to chase their agency for updates or sit through waffle on a call. They want someone who gets to the point, tells them exactly what's happening, and is available when they need them. That's how I operate — no account managers, no ticketing systems, just straight communication.

---

**How do your top competitors claim to be unique?**

AI lead gen agencies doing similar things to us. Aluma Agency targets local trades businesses with AI-powered lead generation, automated follow-up, and appointment booking. They claim "proprietary funnels" and a 5x ROI, with some form of money-back guarantee — though the details are vague. Winter Marketing offer AI phone agents, voice agents, and generative AI integration for SMBs, and they demo their tech live which is a decent trust-builder. The I Need Group have an AI chatbot called "AGENT" and position themselves as an all-in-one platform with sector-specific funnels for solar, home improvement, etc. They promise "fully booked schedules" and "exponential growth" — heavy on the hype language.

Bournemouth generalist agencies. LOCALiQ, boxChilli, Edirect, CuCo Creative — these are traditional digital marketing agencies offering SEO, PPC, web design, and social media. They serve local businesses but none of them are doing what we do with custom AI appointment booking. Their claims are standard: "data-driven," "proven results," "measurable ROI." Nothing that specifically solves the problem of missed leads, slow follow-up, or automated booking.

SaaS tools that businesses could use instead of an agency. Setter AI, SiteGPT, Podium — these are self-serve platforms offering AI chatbots, WhatsApp booking, and automated lead follow-up. They're cheap and accessible, but they require the business owner to set it up, manage it, and run their own ads. No strategy, no campaign management, no one building it for them.

The common thread across all of them: big promises, vague specifics, and guarantee claims designed to sound risk-free but almost certainly loaded with T&Cs that make them hard to actually claim on.

---

**Taking the above into consideration, what do you think your agency could do differently?**

Most competitors are either all tech and no strategy, or all promises and no substance. We sit in a gap that none of them fill properly.

The AI lead gen agencies sell automation but most of them are reselling the same white-label platforms. Their "proprietary funnels" are usually Go High Level with a skin on it. They can't customise beyond what the tool allows. We build with custom Python, which means if a client's business needs the AI to qualify leads based on specific criteria — say, minimum spend, property type, both homeowners present — we build that in. We're not limited by what some off-the-shelf chatbot can and can't do.

The SaaS tools are cheap, but cheap and good value are two very different things. A £100/month tool still doesn't generate a single lead — the business owner needs to run their own ads. Then they've got to integrate it into their calendar, their CRM, their systems. Then they've got to configure the conversations, test them, tweak them, and hope it doesn't sound robotic. They're buying a tool and becoming their own agency. We take all of that off their plate.

The biggest thing we do differently is harder to copy: I've spent years in telesales and face-to-face sales, so I actually understand how a sales conversation needs to flow. That directly shapes how we build the AI booking agents — the way they qualify, the way they handle objections, the way they guide someone toward booking. Most agencies are built by marketers who've never had to close a deal. That shows in the quality of what they build.

The Bournemouth generalists — SEO agencies, web designers, PPC shops — aren't really our competition. They're not solving the same problem. A local business can have the best website in the world, but if they're missing leads because nobody's following up fast enough or they're on a job when the phone rings, it doesn't matter. That's the gap we fill.

## Crafting Your NSVP

Define the core value your agency offers to your ICP by outlining the benefits, solutions, and transformative impact of your services.

---

**What are the main benefits and solutions your agency provides to address the unique challenges of your niche?**

Local service businesses — roofers, solar installers, fascia and guttering companies — are all dealing with the same problems. They don't know where to start with marketing, or what they've tried hasn't worked. They've stayed up until midnight watching YouTube tutorials trying to figure out Facebook Ads and they're still not getting the right people coming through. On top of that, they can't answer the phone half the time — they're up a ladder, they're on a job, they're having dinner with their family at 7pm when a lead comes in. By the time they call back the next morning, that lead's already gone with someone else.

We solve all of it.

We run targeted lead generation ads through Meta to generate a consistent flow of enquiries from people who actually need the service. Those leads are then handled by our custom-built AI booking agent, which qualifies them against the client's specific criteria — not just "are you interested?" but real qualifying questions like budget, property type, timeline, decision-makers. The AI pre-sells the appointment so by the time the prospect shows up, they're informed, qualified, and far easier to close.

On top of that, we completely revamp their website so it actually converts visitors into enquiries rather than just sitting there looking pretty. We install AI-powered missed call text back — so if a lead calls and the business can't answer, they instantly receive a WhatsApp and text message, can ask questions through the AI, and book an appointment without the business owner having to lift a finger. We also help them build up their Google reviews, which strengthens their local presence and makes them the obvious choice when someone's searching in their area.

The result: instead of Monday morning being a scramble to chase cold leads, call back people who've already gone elsewhere, and try to figure out how to grow while still doing the actual work — they just go and do jobs. Their pipeline's filling up. Appointments are being booked in automatically. They get a notification saying "new appointment booked" and they carry on with their day.

---

**How will your agency elevate your clients' success?**

We take local service businesses from stressed, reactive, and inconsistent — to structured, scaling, and confident.

Before working with us, most of these business owners are doing everything themselves. They're on the tools all day, chasing leads in the evening, worrying about whether enough work is coming in to cover wages at the end of the month. They're turning up to quotes where the homeowner is shopping around five different companies and has no real intent to move forward. Every missed call is a missed opportunity, and they know it.

After working with us, that changes. Leads are coming in consistently. Every single one gets followed up with instantly — no missed opportunities, no leads going cold overnight. The AI qualifies them properly: what's their budget, why are they looking at this now, have they explored it before, are all decision-makers involved? By the time the business owner turns up to quote a job, the prospect has already had their questions answered, they've been pre-sold on the value, and they're showing up with genuine intent to move forward. That changes the close rate completely — these aren't tyre-kickers, they're informed buyers.

We also follow up before every appointment to keep the no-show rate low. As we build up a track record with the client, we can feed real testimonials and case studies into the AI's conversations, which strengthens that pre-sell even further over time.

The bigger picture: their pipeline fills up, they stop worrying about where the next job's coming from, and they start thinking about growth — hiring, expanding their service area, reinvesting. They go from being a local tradesperson who does a bit of marketing to being a market leader running an AI-first business. That shift in how they see themselves and how their customers see them is where the real elevation happens.

We help local service businesses generate a consistent flow of high-intent, pre-qualified, booked appointments using custom-built AI, while building them into the highest-rated, most visible business in their area so they become the obvious choice.

## Building Your Killer Offer

This section focuses on crafting an irresistible offer for your Ideal Client Profile (ICP).

---

**What timeframe do you plan to be able to deliver results to your clients?**

The full system — website revamp, AI booking agent, missed call text back, reviews system, and ad campaigns — goes live within 10 days of onboarding.

Month one is live and generating leads, but it's a testing period. Leads will come in, appointments will get booked, but we're gathering data — learning what messaging works, what targeting converts, and refining the AI based on real feedback from the client. We don't pretend month one is the finished product.

By month two to three, the system is properly dialled in. Targeting is tighter, the AI's qualification is refined to the client's specific criteria, and appointments are coming through at a consistent pace. Based on realistic cost-per-lead ranges for local service businesses (£10–£50 depending on the market) and a minimum ad budget of £750/month, we plan to generate 5–15 high-intent, pre-qualified booked appointments per month.

We don't guarantee a specific number upfront. We'd rather be honest about what's realistic, deliver on it, and then tighten the numbers once we've got initial results to work from. The market moves, things change, and we'd rather understate and overdeliver than make promises we can't back up. That's not how we operate.

---

**How will your pricing strategy align with the perceived value and needs of your ICP?**

Our pricing is built around the size of the business and ranges from £500–£1,500 per month, with an average of around £1,000. This covers the full service: targeted Meta ad campaigns, custom AI booking agent, missed call text back, website revamp, and Google review collection. On top of this, clients commit to a minimum ad spend of £750/month, putting their total monthly investment at roughly £1,250–£2,250.

We work on 3-month contracts. After that, the client either re-signs for another 3 months or walks — no penalties, no lock-in beyond the agreed term. We keep clients because the system works, not because they're stuck.

For context, a single roofing job can be worth £5,000+, a solar installation £8,000+. Even at the lower end — fascia and guttering at £1,000–£2,000 a job — if we're generating 5–15 booked appointments per month and the client closes even a fraction of those, the ROI pays for itself many times over. We don't need to oversell the maths, it speaks for itself.

In the early stages, while we build our case studies and testimonials, we'll offer a free trial where the client covers their own ad spend and receives the full service at no management fee. This lets us prove what the system can do with real results, and gives the client zero risk in trying it. Once we have proven results and niche down further, we plan to move pricing to £2,000/month, backed by data and case studies that justify it.

---

**What extra value can you provide to make your offer irresistible?**

Most of what we offer is already more than what competitors include at the same price point. The full service covers targeted Meta ads, a custom-built AI booking agent, website revamp, missed call text back with AI follow-up, weekly reports, monthly strategy calls, and direct WhatsApp access. Most agencies charge separately for half of those things.

On top of that, we include an automated AI review collection system. As soon as a job is completed — when the customer is at their happiest — they receive a WhatsApp message (with SMS fallback if it doesn't deliver) nudging them to leave a Google review with a direct link. It's well-timed, it's automated, and it compounds over time. More reviews means higher Google rankings, more trust, and more organic leads coming in alongside the paid ones.

The real kicker: on day one, before a single ad has gone live, if a client has an existing customer list — say 100 past jobs — we can immediately send out review requests to that entire list through the AI. That means they start climbing Google from the moment they sign up, not three months down the line. That's instant, tangible value before the ad campaigns have even launched.

When clients see the full picture — the ads, the AI agent, the website, the missed call system, the reviews, the direct access — they realise they're not just getting a lead gen service. They're getting an entire growth system.

---

**What will you offer as a risk reversal and/or guarantee if you cannot deliver on your promise?**

We work on 3-month contracts with a clean exit — no lock-in beyond the term. If it's not working, they walk. We also offer free trials in the early stages where the client covers ad spend only and receives the full service at no management fee. If we don't deliver on what we've agreed, we'll keep working for free until we do — but that's a sales call closer, not a marketing headline. We use it as a last resort, not a lead magnet.
